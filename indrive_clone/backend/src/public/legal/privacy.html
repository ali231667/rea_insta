Privacy Policy

Student project – not affiliated with inDrive. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

Phone number, name, email (optional), profile photo, ride history, ratings, chat messages during a ride, and your location while you use the app. For drivers also: CNIC, driving license, vehicle details and document photos.

Why we collect it

To log you in, find nearby drivers, show live location during a ride, calculate prices, keep you safe (SOS, trip sharing), help with support requests, and prevent fraud.

Location

Passengers share location to set the pickup point and during an active ride. Drivers share location while they are online, also when the app is in the background, so passengers can see the car coming. You can turn off location in your phone settings, but the app will not work fully.

Who can see your data

During a ride, the passenger and the driver see each other's name, photo, rating and phone number. Phone numbers are hidden before a driver is chosen. Our support team can see ride data to help you. We do not sell your data.

Services we use

OpenStreetMap / Google Maps for maps and routes, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications, and an SMS provider for login codes.

How long we keep data

Ride history is kept for safety and legal reasons. When you delete your account, we remove your name, phone, email and photo; anonymous ride records stay.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, and delete your account in Settings → Delete account. For other requests contact support.

Security

Passwords are not used; logins use one-time SMS codes and short-lived tokens stored in your phone's secure storage. Connections should use HTTPS in production.

Children

The app is not for people under 18.

Contact

Use Help → Contact support in the app.
