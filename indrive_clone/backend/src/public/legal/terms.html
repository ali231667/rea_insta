Student project – not affiliated with inDrive. Last updated: October 2026.

1. About the service

inDrive clone is a student project that connects passengers with independent drivers. Passengers offer a price, drivers can accept it or offer another price, and the passenger chooses a driver. We are a technology platform, not a transport company.

2. Your account

You need a Pakistani mobile number to use the app. Keep your phone safe: anyone with your phone can use your account. You must be 18 years or older. Give true information (name, documents).

3. Prices and payment

The fare is agreed between the passenger and the driver before the trip. The passenger pays the driver in cash at the end of the trip. Drivers pay the platform a commission (10%) from their in-app balance.

4. Drivers

Drivers must have a valid CNIC, driving license and a vehicle in good condition. Drivers are responsible for following traffic laws. We can block drivers who break the rules or get many low ratings.

5. Behaviour

Be respectful. No violence, harassment, discrimination, smoking without permission, or illegal items. Report problems with the Help screen or the Safety button. We may block accounts that break these rules.

6. Cancellations

You can cancel a request before the trip starts. Please give a reason. Many cancellations can lead to a block.

7. Safety

In an emergency call the police (15) or Rescue (1122). The SOS button in the app also alerts our team. Always check the car plate before you get in.
